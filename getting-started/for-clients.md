# For Clients: What You Get

A complete, production-ready learning platform deployed in **your** AWS account, in **3 weeks**, with a proven delivery playbook.

## The package

✅ **Full platform** — learner app, admin console, course engine, branded to your identity
✅ **Infrastructure as code** — your entire AWS stack in Terraform, reviewable and auditable by your IT team
✅ **Documentation** — this site: architecture, runbooks, operations guides
✅ **Knowledge transfer** — your team learns the platform during the deployment
✅ **Security baseline** — hardened after a full independent audit (Aug 2026)

## Your responsibilities (the prerequisites)

To hit the 3-week timeline, we need from you (detailed in [Client Prerequisites](../deployment/client-prerequisites.md)):

1. **A dedicated AWS sub-account** — isolates the project, simplifies billing, you keep full control
2. **DNS delegation** — a subdomain like `learn.yourcompany.com`
3. **Brand assets** — logos, colors, typography
4. **Named contacts** — IT, security, and a project sponsor

## What running it costs

Typical monthly AWS cost for a pilot (100–1,000 active learners): **$225–300/month**, on your own AWS bill. No per-user license fees from us.

## After go-live

- **You own everything** — code, infrastructure, data
- **Optional support** — we offer maintenance retainers, or your team takes over fully (the runbooks make it possible)
- **Product upgrades** — new platform capabilities (see [Roadmap](../roadmap/README.md)) can be rolled into your deployment
