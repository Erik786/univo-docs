## 📋 What we need from you

To hit the timeline, four things (detailed in [Client Prerequisites](../deployment/client-prerequisites.md)):

1. **☁️ A dedicated AWS sub-account**, isolates the project, simplifies billing, you keep full control
2. **🌐 DNS delegation**, a subdomain like `learn.yourcompany.com`
3. **🎨 Brand assets**, logos, colors, typography
4. **👤 Named contacts**, IT, security, and a project sponsor
